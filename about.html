About Valko's Lily Donations

The Valko Lily Donations is a project organized by the fundraising team over on the ValkoHQ Discord server, and is spread through social platforms. The project aims to redirect the players' negative emotions to spreading love and positivity instead via donations to various charities, and ultimately strengthen the overall impact of the boycott. It shares the important message:

Love is stronger than hate.

While the project was started to cater to Valko's image, it now also caters to other LIs as well. Out of respect for every LI impacted by this situation, the Valko Lily Donations have started spreading new lists of charities. These charities are themed solely around the affected LI and provide various ways of spending for the LI's cause while continuing to support the boycott, all while giving the LI the dedication they deserve amidst the ongoing negativity.

This is their "thank you" to all mains who have unfortunately been wronged during this situation, with the hope that doing this small act will help aid and ease some of the hurt and negative feelings they may be facing. Donating to charities in their LI's name will help spread their love to the rest of the world.
