Animal Welfare
6 organizations

Supporting animal shelters and rescue efforts around the world.

Hope For Strays

a registered non-profit in Thailand (East Pattaya) that feeds, provides medical care for, and sterilizes stray dogs, and runs a shelter currently housing 300 dogs.

Second Chance Canine Rescue

a non-profit dog rescue based in the Chicago area, Illinois. It rescues homeless dogs, places them in foster homes rather than kennels, and works to find each one a permanent, well-matched home.

Donate →

Pawscrossed Family

a 501(c)3 pet adoption center based in Elmsford, NY. It rescues, rehabilitates, and rehomes orphaned dogs and cats under the mission “Rescue One By One Until There Are None.”

Donate →

Refugio Angelitos de Edgar (Bolivia)

an animal shelter in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia, founded and run by Édgar Ortega. It houses over 300 rescued stray dogs and cats, run largely through volunteers and community donations.

Animal Shelter in Latvia — Ar sirdi delnā

Caring for around 200 dogs and cats. It has recently faced serious financial hardship, including having its electricity cut off due to unpaid bills, and relies heavily on public donations to keep operating.

Saving Cats in China

a volunteer-led group, based primarily in China with supporters worldwide, focused on rescuing cats and exposing the cat/dog meat trade in Asia; donations go to shelters through their partner network.
